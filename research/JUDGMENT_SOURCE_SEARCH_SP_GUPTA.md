The AWS Registry of Open Data describes that dataset as sourced from eCourts and licensed CC-BY-4.0. This is a third-party dataset copy, not a direct download from a Supreme Court-hosted endpoint. The text says consultation implies a conference of two or more persons or an impact of two or more minds, then describes exchange of full and identical facts and discussion of respective views. PDF page 607 continues the opinion under the printed heading ?S.P. Gupta v. Union (Tulpazapurkar, J.)?.
